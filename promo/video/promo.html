Municipal Ordinance No. 105

What the law says about fares

Sec. 2Covers every tricycle and motorcycle for hire operating in Basey.
Sec. 7Only the Sangguniang Bayan sets and adjusts fares — after a public hearing.
Sec. 24Fares are per passenger, with a discount for students, seniors and PWDs.
Sec. 19(k)Drivers charge the approved fare — nothing more.
Sec. 19(n)The approved fare is posted where passengers can see it.
Sec. 29–30Violations are grounds to cancel the franchise, decided by the Sangguniang Bayan.
Discounts backed by national law

Your data, protected

Built to stay lawful

The app enforces the ordinance — it never rewrites it.

Verified routes only

If a road route can't be confirmed, no official fare is issued. No guesses on the record.

Fare changes need an issuance

Staff publish a new rate only with the Sangguniang Bayan document behind it, on its effective date.

Penalties cite their section

Fines only where the ordinance authorises one. Overcharging goes to the franchise process under Sec. 29–30.
